About Fair Deal Properties

Built on the conviction that your capital deserves more than a listing.

Fair Deal Properties Ltd is an integrated real estate, PropTech and transaction facilitation company delivering trusted property solutions through professional expertise, digital innovation, verified market intelligence and funding advisory.

Fair Deal Properties Ltd
Institutional discipline, applied to private property advisory.
Our Mission

Structure. Verification.
Stewardship.

We exist to serve buyers, investors, developers, and diaspora Nigerians who are serious about property as capital - not just as a place to live.

We are a technology-enabled real estate agency and transaction facilitation firm. We combine professional advisory, verified property opportunities, digital platforms and market intelligence to make property transactions more transparent, efficient, secure and accessible. Technology is our strategic enabler - the means by which we differentiate service, improve client experience and reduce transaction risk across how property is marketed, acquired, developed, financed, managed and experienced.

Nigeria's property market has tremendous opportunity but real structural hazards: fake listings, title ambiguity, weak due diligence, and financing uncertainty. Our model is built explicitly to navigate and neutralise each of these on your behalf - with institutional rigour applied to every transaction we touch.

Our Operating Principles

  • We list nothing we have not independently verified.
  • Title clarity is non-negotiable - always.
  • Advisory precedes every transaction.
  • Diaspora clients receive dedicated stewardship - without exception.
  • Post-transaction support is part of the engagement, not an add-on.
  • Integrity before incentive - always.
Our Values

How we operate - every time.

Integrity Before Incentive

Our advisory is not commission-driven. We are structured to prioritise what is right for you, not what earns us the fastest fee. Our reputation is built on this commitment.

Precision Over Speed

Every verified opportunity demands rigorous review. We would rather lose a listing than compromise our verification standard for urgency. Good decisions take proper time.

Transparency as Infrastructure

You will know what we know. No hidden fees, no undisclosed relationships, no selective omissions. Transparency is the foundation of every client relationship we build.

Long-Term Partnership

Our relationship does not end at title transfer. We are invested in the performance and protection of your asset for years beyond the transaction. This is who we are.
