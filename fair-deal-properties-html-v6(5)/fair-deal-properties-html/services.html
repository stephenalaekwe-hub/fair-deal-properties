Our Services

Five ways we structure and protect every client engagement.

Each service has been designed around the real challenges clients face in Nigeria's property market - not around what is easiest for us to offer.

Service One

Real Estate Agency & Transaction Facilitation

End-to-end transaction management, not just introductions.

We manage the complete transaction lifecycle - from opportunity identification and site inspection to offer negotiation, legal documentation, and title transfer. Our involvement provides a structured layer of discipline that protects your interest at every stage. We work only with verified listings, ensuring no transaction begins without clear documentary evidence of title and ownership.

Speak to an Advisor

What's Included

  • Pre-market opportunity sourcing
  • Independent market price analysis
  • Offer structuring and negotiation
  • Legal coordination and document review
  • Title transfer support and post-close monitoring
Service Two

Real Estate Investment Advisory

Capital allocation counsel, not commission-driven brokerage.

Our investment advisory service is structured for clients who approach property as a capital decision - not just a lifestyle purchase. We provide market intelligence, opportunity analysis, and structured guidance to help you build a considered position in the Nigerian property market. Our advisory is not commission-driven - it is built around your outcome.

Speak to an Advisor

What's Included

  • Market intelligence and sector outlook
  • Investment thesis development
  • Opportunity screening and shortlisting
  • Risk and return analysis
  • Portfolio composition guidance
Service Three

Bridge-Financing Facilitation & Financial Advisory

Connecting capital-ready buyers with structured financing channels.

Financing is often the single largest barrier to completing a sound property transaction. We facilitate access to bridge-financing solutions and maintain relationships with credible lending partners. We do not lend - but we advise, introduce, and structure. Our role is to help you access the right financing instrument for your situation, with full transparency on terms and obligations.

Explore Financing Advisory

What's Included

  • Bridge-financing needs assessment
  • Lender introductions and facilitation
  • Financing term review and independent guidance
  • Debt structure advisory
  • Post-financing transaction support
