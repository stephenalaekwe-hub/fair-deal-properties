Fee disclosure and basis. These are Fair Deal Properties' commercial rates. They are not a representation that any percentage is prescribed by law. The applicable rate within each band is fixed in writing at engagement by reference to asset class, mandate type, transaction complexity and value. Only one primary fee basis applies to any single transaction; where more than one service is rendered, the fee structure is set out in a single schedule so that nothing is charged twice. Fair Deal Properties Ltd receives no undisclosed commission from any developer, vendor, lender or third party. Where a fee is receivable from more than one party to the same transaction, that arrangement is disclosed to all parties in writing before the engagement proceeds and is recorded in our conflict of interest register. Assurance and verification fees in section B are payable whether or not a transaction proceeds and are never contingent on completion.
