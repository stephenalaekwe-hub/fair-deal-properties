How It Works

A structured process. No ambiguity at any stage.

From first conversation to post-transfer stewardship - six disciplined steps that govern every engagement, every time. No shortcuts. No surprises.

The Engagement Process
Six steps. No ambiguity at any stage.
01
Confidential Consultation

A structured advisory session - in person, by video, or by phone - to understand your goals, timeline, risk profile, budget, and specific constraints. We listen before we recommend. This session is obligation-free and completely confidential.

02
Opportunity Screening & Mandate Definition

We identify and screen opportunities against your defined mandate - from our verified platform and off-market sources. You receive only what passes our standard. This is curated shortlisting, not mass listing distribution.

03
Independent Due Diligence

Before any commitment is made, we conduct an independent title search, document review, site verification, and developer assessment where applicable. You receive a written verification report from our legal team. No naira moves before this report is delivered and reviewed.

04
Offer Structuring & Negotiation

We represent your interest at the negotiating table - structuring the offer, negotiating terms, managing counterparty expectations, and ensuring your contractual protections are embedded in every agreement. You never negotiate alone.

05
Legal Coordination & Title Transfer

Full legal coordination - Deed of Assignment drafting, Governor's Consent, title registration where applicable, payment milestone management, and final handover. Every document is verified before it is executed. Every payment is tracked and confirmed.

06
Post-Transfer Stewardship

The engagement does not end at title transfer. We provide continued advisory, asset monitoring, rental facilitation referrals, and quarterly intelligence reports for as long as you hold the asset. Your investment is our long-term responsibility.
