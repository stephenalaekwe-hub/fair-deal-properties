What a virtual walkthrough is and is not. A virtual walkthrough presents dated evidence captured by a named Fair Deal Properties officer who physically attended the site on the stated date. It records what was observed on that date. It does not replace a physical inspection, a title investigation or an assurance report, and it is not a representation about title, ownership or condition at any other date. Every session is logged and the log is available to the client on request.

Physical Inspection

A named officer attends. You track every step.

Request a physical inspection on any property, whether or not it is on our platform. A named field officer attends, completes the standard Site Inspection Checklist in full, and captures dated, geotagged evidence. You receive notifications at each stage and a tracking reference you can check at any time.

What the officer does on site

  • Geotagged arrival photograph with time on site recorded
  • Coordinates recorded and matched against the survey plan
  • Beacon numbers sighted, photographed, boundary walked
  • Photographs from a minimum of four aspects, plus video
  • Infrastructure, access, occupancy and encroachment recorded
  • Every discrepancy against documentation described and photographed

Strictly necessary Always active
Required for the site to function - page navigation, form submission, session continuity and recording your choice on this notice. These are set on the basis of legitimate interest and cannot be disabled. No personally identifying profile is built from them.
Functional and preference
Remembers choices you make - saved properties, the page you were last on, your inspection session reference. Retention up to 12 months. Declining these means the site will not remember your choices between visits.
