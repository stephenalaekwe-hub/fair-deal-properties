Data Protection

How your information is protected

NDPA 2023 Compliance

We process personal data as a Data Controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, with a lawful basis identified for every processing activity. Our full Privacy Notice sets out your data subject rights and how to exercise them.

Access Segregation

Verification staff, advisory staff and finance staff hold separate permissions. No single role can originate, verify and approve the same transaction - the same control principle applied in financial services.

Audit Trail Integrity

Verification findings, document access and transaction milestones are written to an append-only log. Records can be added to and read, but not silently altered or deleted.

Retention Discipline

Transaction records retained 7 years, KYC records 5 years post-relationship, in line with Nigerian tax and AML obligations. Enquiry data not converted to engagement is purged after 2 years.

Regulatory Framework

The rules we operate under

We would rather state our regulatory boundaries plainly than let a client assume we do something we are not authorised to do.

Framework
What It Means For You
MLPPA 2022 & SCUML
Real estate businesses are designated non-financial institutions. All clients complete KYC verification before engagement. We are required to report suspicious transactions without prior notice to the client.
NDPA 2023 / NDPC
Registered data controller obligations, data subject rights honoured within 30 days, and mandatory breach notification.
ESVARBON
We do not offer valuation services. Formal property valuation is reserved to registered Estate Surveyors and Valuers. Where a valuation is required we engage a registered firm on your behalf. Our market analytics are decision intelligence, clearly distinguished from valuation.
Mortgage Facilitation
We facilitate and advise on mortgage arrangements with licensed institutions. We are not a lender, we do not originate credit in our own name, and we do not take deposits or hold client funds.

On AI and honesty: We use artificial intelligence to draft documents, structure data, match opportunities and automate follow-up. We do not use it to generate verification findings, title conclusions, property condition assessments or client testimonials. In a market where anyone can now generate a convincing listing, the scarce asset is a person who actually visited the site and put their name to what they found. That is what we sell.
