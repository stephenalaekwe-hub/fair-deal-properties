Financing Advisory

We don't lend. We ensure you borrow right.

Financing is often the single largest barrier to a sound property transaction. We advise, introduce, and structure - with full transparency on terms and obligations, always.

Financing Routes
NHF, MREIF, PMI and commercial - compared on total cost, not headline rate.
Our Financing Advisory Service

Structure before you commit.

We assess your financing requirements early in the advisory process - before you are in negotiation and under pressure. Our role is to ensure you never commit to financing terms you don't fully understand.

We maintain relationships with credible bridge-financing providers across Nigeria's formal and informal capital markets. We introduce only where the terms are transparent, the provider is credible, and the structure serves your long-term interest - not theirs.
