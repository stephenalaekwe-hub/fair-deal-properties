Article 1

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are widely used to make sites work, to remember your preferences, and in some cases to collect information about how a site is used.

This notice also covers technologies that work in a similar way, including web beacons, pixel tags, local storage and session storage. Where we refer to cookies we include all of these.

Article 2

Our consent position

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, consent must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous, and must be as easy to withdraw as it is to give. We apply that as follows:

  • No optional cookie is set before you consent. Our consent banner does not pre-tick any category, and closing or ignoring it is not treated as consent.
  • Consent is granular. You may accept one category and refuse another. Accepting all is never required in order to refuse all.
  • Rejecting is as easy as accepting. Both are a single click, presented with equal prominence.
  • Consent is not a condition of use. Every part of this site works whether you accept optional cookies or not.
  • Your consent is recorded. We store what you chose, when, by what method and against which version of this notice, so that we can evidence it. You may view that record at any time using the control above.
  • Withdrawal is always available. The privacy control at the lower left of every page reopens the preference centre.

Article 4

Third-party cookies

We operate no third-party advertising or tracking network on this site.

This site loads typefaces from a third-party font service. That request may expose your internet protocol address to the provider. If you refuse optional cookies, fonts still load, because the request is part of rendering the page rather than a cookie. If you wish to prevent it entirely, your browser or an extension can block third-party font loading; the site remains fully readable in system typefaces.

Where we engage any third-party processor in future, we will conduct due diligence on its data protection standards, bind it by a written data processing agreement, and update this notice before deployment.

Links from this site to third-party websites are outside our control. Those sites operate their own cookie practices and we accept no responsibility for them.

Article 5

Your controls

  • Preference centre - the primary control. Set each category independently. Reachable from the banner, from the privacy control at the lower left of every page, and from the buttons at the top of this notice.
  • Withdraw entirely - clears the consent record from your device and restores the banner, so you may choose afresh.
  • View your record - shows what you chose, when, by what method and against which notice version.
  • Browser settings - most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Disabling all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, will impair this and most other websites.
  • Contact us - our Data Protection Officer at dpo@fairdealng.com will action any request about cookie data.
Article 6

Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least annually and whenever we change the cookies we use. Where a change introduces a new category or a new purpose, we will seek fresh consent rather than rely on consent given against an earlier version. The version number above identifies the notice against which your consent was recorded.
