Nothing optional is placed on your device until you choose below. You may decline everything optional and continue using the site normally.

Strictly necessary Always active
Required for the site to function - page navigation, form submission, session continuity and recording your choice on this notice. These are set on the basis of legitimate interest and cannot be disabled. No personally identifying profile is built from them.
Functional and preference
Remembers choices you make - saved properties, the page you were last on, your inspection session reference. Retention up to 12 months. Declining these means the site will not remember your choices between visits.

Declining is as easy as accepting and carries no disadvantage. You can change your choices at any time using the Privacy Choices control at the bottom left of any page.
