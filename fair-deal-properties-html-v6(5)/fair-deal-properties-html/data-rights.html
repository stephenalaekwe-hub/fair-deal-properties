Your Data Rights

Exercise any right you hold over your personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. The Act requires us to act without undue delay; we commit to acknowledging within 5 business days and responding within 30 days. There is no charge.

What you can ask for

Ten rights, and what each one does

  • Be informed - know what we do with your data. Our Privacy Notice discharges this.
  • Access - receive a copy of your data and information about how we use it.
  • Rectification - have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected without undue delay.
  • Erasure - have data deleted where it is no longer necessary or where consent is withdrawn and no other basis applies.
  • Restriction - limit our processing while a dispute about accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
  • Portability - receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format and have it transmitted onward where feasible.
  • Object - object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to direct marketing at any time without exception.
  • Withdraw consent - at any time, as easily as it was given.
  • Automated decisions - not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effect. We make no such decisions.
  • Complain - to us, and to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Where erasure is limited

Some records we are required by law to keep. Customer due diligence records must be retained for 5 years after the end of a business relationship under the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, and transaction records for 7 years for tax and professional liability purposes. Where we cannot erase, we will tell you precisely which obligation prevents it and when the data will be deleted.

If you are not satisfied with our response

Article 40(2) and (3) of the General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 allow you to serve a Standard Notice to Address Grievance on us, in the form at Schedule 9 to that Directive. You may use that route with us and we will treat it as a formal grievance. Serving it is not a precondition to complaining to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission - you may do either, or both.

If the matter remains unresolved you may escalate to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or institute civil proceedings. We will not require you to exhaust our internal process before approaching the Commission.

Your Privacy Choices
We ask before we set any cookie

Fair Deal Properties Ltd uses cookies on this website. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, cookies that are not strictly necessary require your active consent before they are set. Nothing optional is placed on your device until you choose below. You may decline everything optional and continue using the site normally.

Strictly necessary Always active
Required for the site to function - page navigation, form submission, session continuity and recording your choice on this notice. These are set on the basis of legitimate interest and cannot be disabled. No personally identifying profile is built from them.
Functional and preference
Remembers choices you make - saved properties, the page you were last on, your inspection session reference. Retention up to 12 months. Declining these means the site will not remember your choices between visits.
Analytics and performance
Anonymised information on how the site is used, so we can improve it. Retention up to 24 months. We do not use this to identify you and we do not sell or share it for any third party's own purposes.
Marketing
We do not currently operate advertising or third-party remarketing cookies. If that changes, nothing will be set unless you switch this on. Direct marketing by any channel requires your prior consent and is never bundled with any other permission.
Declining is as easy as accepting and carries no disadvantage. You can change your choices at any time using the Privacy Choices control at the bottom left of any page. Your decision, the categories you selected and the date are recorded as evidence of consent. See our Cookie Policy, Privacy Notice and Data Protection Centre.
